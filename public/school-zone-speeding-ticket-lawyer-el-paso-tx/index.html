School Zone Speeding Ticket Lawyer El Paso, TX

School crossing zone tickets carry doubled fines and no deferred disposition, so the signage, flashing-light and timing defenses matter more than usual.

Fight Your TicketCall 915-200-1133

If you are searching for a school zone speeding ticket lawyer in El Paso, TX, it is probably because you have learned that this is not an ordinary speeding ticket. A citation for an offense committed in a school crossing zone carries doubled fines under Transportation Code §542.404, and Texas law closes off deferred disposition and the statutory right to a driving safety course dismissal for these offenses. The usual ways to keep a ticket off your record are not there. What remains is the question of whether the State can prove the zone was lawfully established, properly signed, and actually in effect when you drove through it.

How a school zone speed limit is created under §545.356

A school zone speed limit is not automatic. Transportation Code §545.356 gives a municipality the authority to alter the prima facie speed limit on streets within its jurisdiction, including setting a lower limit in a school zone, and that authority has to be exercised by ordinance after an engineering and traffic investigation. The lower limit takes effect only when signs giving notice of it are posted. The same structure applies on county roads and state highways through the commissioners court and the Texas Transportation Commission.

That means the State's case has two layers. First, the ordinance or order establishing the zone, its boundaries, its speed, and its hours. Second, the signs on the street that communicate it. A citation is only as good as the weaker of those layers, and we check both. A zone whose posted boundaries or hours do not match the ordinance, or that has not been formally established at all, is a defense the court will hear.

Doubled fines and the closed doors under §542.404

Section 542.404 provides that the fine for an offense committed while operating a vehicle in a school crossing zone is doubled: the minimum and maximum fines are each twice what they would otherwise be. For a Class C speeding offense that normally carries up to $200, the ceiling becomes $400 plus court costs. The same doubling applies to a wireless communication device offense in a school crossing zone.

The larger consequence is procedural. Code of Criminal Procedure art. 45A.301 (formerly art. 45.051) states that deferred disposition does not apply to an offense to which §542.404 applies. The driving safety course subchapter likewise provides that the statutory right to complete a course does not apply to a §542.404 offense. Whether a particular judge will nonetheless permit a course as a matter of discretion varies by court, and we will tell you what to expect in yours, but you should not assume either option is available. In practice, a school zone citation that is paid or pleaded becomes a conviction and a moving violation on your record, and the way to avoid that is to contest it. Flashing lights, signs and hours: the defenses that matter

A school zone limit applies only during the hours it is posted, and the signs are the notice that makes it enforceable. We look closely at:

  • Flashing beacons. Many El Paso zones are posted as effective "when flashing." If the beacons were not operating at the time of the citation, the reduced limit was not in effect. Beacon controllers fail, lose their schedules after power outages, and are sometimes left on the previous year's calendar. We request the City's maintenance and timing records for that beacon.
  • Posted hours. Zones posted with fixed hours apply only during those hours on school days. A citation written on a holiday, a staff development day, or during a summer session that does not match the posted schedule is contestable.
  • Sign placement and condition. The sign that begins the zone must be visible before the point where the officer measured your speed. A sign obscured by vegetation, a parked truck or a construction barricade, or placed after the measurement point, undermines the notice element.
  • Zone boundaries. Officers sometimes set up at the far end of a zone and measure vehicles that are exiting or that are already past the end sign.

On top of the zone-specific issues, every school zone case is still a speed case. The radar, lidar or pacing evidence is subject to the same calibration, certification and target-identification challenges we raise on any speeding ticket.

Cell phone use in a school zone under §545.425

Transportation Code §545.425 makes it an offense to use a wireless communication device while operating a motor vehicle in a school crossing zone, unless the vehicle is stopped or the device is used with a hands-free device. The fine is doubled under §542.404, and the deferred disposition exclusion applies in the same way. The statute also requires the city or county that enforces it to post a sign at the entrance to each school crossing zone informing drivers of the prohibition. When no compliant sign was posted at the entrance you used, we raise that as a defense.

Phone citations are also frequently written on thin observation: an officer at a crosswalk sees a hand raised or a screen glow in a moving vehicle. Whether the device was actually in use, whether the vehicle was stopped in the pickup line, and whether a hands-free function was engaged are all fact questions, and the State carries the burden on each. Phone records and vehicle infotainment logs sometimes resolve them in your favor.

Insurance and license consequences of a school zone conviction

A school zone speeding conviction is reported as a moving violation on your Texas driving record, and insurers in Texas may treat it the same as, or more seriously than, an ordinary speeding conviction because of where it occurred. The Driver Responsibility Program surcharges were repealed on September 1, 2019, so there are no state surcharges, but the Department of Public Safety may still pursue suspension of a license for repeated moving-violation convictions, generally four within 12 months or seven within 24.

If you drive for work, hold a commercial driver's license, or are under 25, the stakes are higher still. CDL holders should read our CDL violation page; the same anti-masking rules apply, and a school zone speed of 15 or more over the limit is also a serious traffic violation under federal regulations.

Frequently asked questions

Can I take defensive driving for a school zone ticket in Texas?

Not as a matter of right. The driving safety course statute excludes offenses to which §542.404, the school crossing zone enhancement, applies, and deferred disposition is excluded as well. Some courts may still consider a course at the judge's discretion; we will tell you how the court handling your case approaches it. Contesting the citation is the primary path.

How much is a school zone speeding ticket in El Paso?

Section 542.404 doubles both the minimum and maximum fine. A Class C speeding offense that would otherwise carry up to $200 carries up to $400 in a school crossing zone, plus court costs. The conviction also goes on your driving record as a moving violation.

The school zone lights were not flashing. Is that a defense?

If the zone is posted as effective when flashing and the beacons were not operating, the reduced limit was not in effect at that moment. We request the City's beacon records to establish it. The ordinary speed limit still applies, so the question becomes whether you exceeded that.

I was ticketed for using my phone in a school zone. What are my options?

Section 545.425 prohibits hand-held device use in a school crossing zone unless the vehicle is stopped or a hands-free device is used, and it requires signs at the zone entrance. Missing signage, a stopped vehicle, hands-free use, and the quality of the officer's observation are the defenses we evaluate. The fine is doubled and deferred disposition is not available.

Does a school zone ticket affect my insurance more than a regular speeding ticket?

It is reported as a moving violation like any speeding conviction, and some insurers weight it more heavily because of the location. Either way, a conviction can raise premiums for several years, which is usually more than the doubled fine.
