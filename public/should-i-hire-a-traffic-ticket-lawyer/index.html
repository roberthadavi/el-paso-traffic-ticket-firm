Should I Hire a Traffic Ticket Lawyer?

Why an El Paso traffic ticket lawyer is often cheaper than paying the ticket

So why is a traffic ticket lawyer so necessary sometimes?

The last thing anyone needs these days is another thing to pay. That is exactly what happens when you get a traffic ticket from a police officer: it is unexpected, it can be a lot of money, and it is stressful because failure to pay will simply increase the amount owed. While the notion of quotas has been disproved in many instances, it still feels as if officers are writing a certain number of tickets. Whatever the reason, the El Paso Police Department, the El Paso County Sheriff's Office and the Texas Department of Public Safety have a significant number of officers dedicated to keeping the roads safe, and they will not hesitate to write a traffic ticket when they believe a traffic law was violated.

What is a traffic violation?

You may be aware of some of these already, but let's go over them. Texting while driving is illegal in Texas and includes using a mobile device in your hand, not just texting. Speeding and driving under the influence are illegal as well. Other common violations are not using child car seats, not wearing a seatbelt, running a red light or stop sign, and driving in El Paso without a valid driver's license — which can be a serious compounding violation. Driving without a license combined with no insurance is one of the more expensive combinations, and if there is an accident it can become a higher-level misdemeanor with possible jail time. Most of these offenses are defined in the Texas Transportation Code.

A valid Texas driver's license is crucial

When it comes to any violation, having a valid driver's license helps your traffic ticket lawyer work toward reducing your fines or resolving your case. We fight for your rights with any ticket you may get. The most common traffic violations in Texas are minor moving violations, and there is almost always a good chance to take your case to court and get a result better than a conviction.

Why not just pay the ticket and move on?

Sometimes simply paying the fine feels like the right mix of civic duty and making the problem go away. But paying is a plea of guilty or no contest. Even once paid, the ticket becomes a conviction on your driving record, which can make other parts of your life more expensive — your car insurance premiums can go up for years, and more severe violations raise them more. Doing the opposite and not paying makes the ticket far more expensive, still puts it on your record, and can lead to a warrant for your arrest with additional fees. If you already have a warrant, don't worry — we can still help. There is effectively no expiration on an unresolved traffic case; it stays with you until it is resolved. We handle old unpaid tickets and warrants as well.

Why use a traffic ticket law firm?

Our firm covers all traffic violations, not just the ones mentioned above: running a red light, not stopping at a stop sign, speeding in a school zone, no-insurance tickets, any moving violation, and anything else related to traffic violations in El Paso. You simply tell us what happened, provide as much information and evidence as you can, and we handle the case — saving your record, your insurance rates, and in serious cases keeping you out of jail. We also work to dismiss warrants if you have unresolved violations and have started receiving failure-to-appear notices. As they pile up and age, those unresolved tickets become very hard to handle on your own without complete knowledge of Texas and El Paso traffic law and detailed documentation.

We work within the law and go beyond simply arguing a ticket is illegible or has inaccurate information. We focus on the actual violation charged and work for a dismissal, deferred disposition or a reduced result every time.

Our experience gives you peace of mind

We are based in El Paso, Texas. We know where tickets are written, which are most common locally, and the best approach to get these violations off your record while saving you money. We provide fixed, transparent pricing that tends to cost less than paying the ticket in full once you count the insurance increases that follow a conviction. Don't let yourself become a victim of a traffic ticket and let crushing fees break your budget.

El Paso Traffic Ticket Firm is the solution

The sooner you reach out, the sooner we can begin to help. We'll tell you whether your case is in the El Paso Municipal Court or a Justice of the Peace court and what needs to be done specifically for your case. At minimum we work to cap how much you will end up paying — especially on past-due unpaid violations — and to keep you from having to serve any jail time. You can pay the ticket as it comes in, head to court when asked and prepare your own defense, or you can spare yourself the headache and let us do the heavy lifting on your Class C misdemeanors, ordinance violations and any other traffic charges. Don't end up paying fees that can easily reach several thousand dollars, especially when you feel you weren't at fault.
