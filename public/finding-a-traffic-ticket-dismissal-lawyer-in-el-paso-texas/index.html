Finding a Traffic Ticket Dismissal Lawyer in El Paso, Texas

Published October 8, 2022 · Updated November 1, 2026 · by Attorney Robert Navar

Finding a good traffic ticket dismissal lawyer in El Paso, Texas — or at least one who will fight your ticket to keep it off your record — can be a tough task. There are many things to consider when looking for a traffic ticket attorney to help fight your tickets. One is how many years of experience your prospective attorney has. This can make a big difference in the outcome of your ticket: an experienced traffic ticket attorney in El Paso will know the ins and outs of the Municipal Court and Justice of the Peace court system, giving you a better chance for a favorable outcome.

Traffic Ticket Attorney Reviews and Customer Service

Another thing to consider is the type of reviews they have received online. Not every review will be favorable, but it is important to see if there is a trend in the reviews the traffic lawyers are receiving. When you call the office, is the staff friendly? Is it easy to get a hold of your traffic ticket attorney for status updates? These are telltale signs of whether that law firm is on top of things.

Remember, an El Paso traffic citation is a Class C misdemeanor, and if not taken care of it can result in a warrant for your arrest. So it is important to make sure whoever you hire is competent and professional. Location can also play a part when trying to track down a ticket dismissal attorney, although many attorneys — including our office — let you take care of everything over the phone, so this may be less important to some.

If you take these things into consideration when trying to find an attorney for your tickets or for warrant dismissals (recalls), you should do fine. Hiring a traffic ticket attorney in El Paso is a convenient way to save you time and, more often than not, money.
