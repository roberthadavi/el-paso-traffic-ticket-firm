Defensive Driving In El Paso, Texas

Published October 8, 2022 · Updated November 1, 2026 · by Attorney Robert Navar

Defensive Driving Lawyer for Traffic Tickets

Defensive driving classes are one way for you to avoid having a traffic conviction on your license. In many cases, taking a Texas defensive driving course can reduce or eliminate the consequences of a traffic violation — for example, points on your driving record and increased insurance rates. Working with top-rated traffic ticket lawyers in El Paso, TX can protect you throughout the process. Defensive driving classes are not your only option if your case is the right candidate for other strategies. For many drivers, however, a driving safety course can limit court costs, keep insurance premiums down, and preserve your driving record. The only way to know you are taking the most advantageous approach is by talking to a traffic ticket lawyer about defensive driving in El Paso, TX.

I Will Help You Beat Your Traffic Citation Through Defensive Driving or Traffic Ticket Dismissal

No matter what type of citation you received, my office wants to help resolve it. Your traffic ticket is defensible, which means you should not automatically pay it before exercising your other options. Once a conviction is there, it stays on your record for years. A defensive driving safety course is one way to obtain a traffic ticket dismissal: it satisfies the court's requirements, keeps the conviction off your record, and may even earn you an insurance discount. While spending six hours in traffic school may sound undesirable, the alternative may be worse. When you hire me to fight your ticket, I will first work toward getting the case dismissed without defensive driving. If that is not available, a driving safety course is an excellent way to clear the ticket and relearn techniques that make you a safer driver.

Find Out If Your Ticket Is Eligible for Dismissal Through a Driving Safety Course

A driving safety course promotes defensive driving practices and runs for six hours. You can take it to dismiss your traffic ticket if your case meets specific criteria, and if you complete it the court dismisses the case. In general, the following TDLR guidelines apply when determining eligibility:

  • No defensive driving dismissal within the last 12 months
  • For speeding tickets, you must not have been going 25 mph or more over the limit (or 95 mph or more)
  • Must have a valid Texas driver's license and proof of insurance
  • Must request the course by your appearance date and complete it within 90 days
  • Must provide the course certificate and a certified copy of your driving record
  • Commercial driver's license holders are not eligible
  • Not available for passing a stopped school bus, leaving the scene of an accident, or speeding in a construction zone with workers present
  • Additional rules as applicable

I understand that your driving record is on the line, along with the consequences it can have on your future, reputation, and finances. That is why I fight for the best possible outcome for my clients every time, and why I make the process as streamlined and stress-free as possible.

You DON'T Have to Admit Guilt to a Citation You Did or Did Not Commit

To take a defensive driving course you must enter a plea of "guilty" or "no contest." Pleading guilty means you admit fault; no contest accepts the result without self-incrimination. I will advise you of the best plea to enter when you hire my office — clients typically plead no contest, and with a completed course the case is dismissed rather than convicted.

The Most Significant Issue with Driver Safety Courses

If you do not complete the course on time, the court can enter the conviction and report it to the Texas Department of Public Safety, which maintains driving records. Deadlines matter.

Fighting Citations You Should Never Have Received

If I believe my office can get your ticket dismissed outright, I will tell you. Instead of paying for and spending six hours in class, you can fight back against a wrongly issued citation. Your time and money are important, and the course fee plus court costs are not always a desirable outcome for my clients.

Sometimes Defensive Driving School Is the Only Option

Some cases are not eligible for dismissal through a driving safety course. If this applies to you, I will help you work through your case, including defending the original citation or pursuing deferred disposition. If you do attend driver safety school, send your certificate to the court as soon as possible so your case is dismissed faster.

Commercial driver's license holders are not eligible for a driving safety course or deferred adjudication, but you can still fight for your ticket's dismissal. If you were traveling through Texas and received a traffic violation, you may not be eligible for a Texas driving safety course, but I can still represent your interests in the El Paso Municipal Court or the court handling your case.
