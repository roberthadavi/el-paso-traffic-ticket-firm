How we handle an El Paso traffic ticket

  1. Free case evaluation. Call or send the citation online. We check every El Paso court for other tickets or warrants under your name at no charge.
  2. Flat fee, quoted up front. You know the cost before we start. Most clients find it is less than the fine plus the insurance increase a conviction causes.
  3. We appear for you. We file our appearance so you do not miss work or school, request discovery, and pursue a dismissal, deferred disposition or driving safety course — whichever protects your record.
  4. You get updates until it's closed. By phone or email, your choice.
