Fight Your Ticket

Hire an El Paso traffic ticket lawyer online — flat fee, no court appearance for you

What happens next

  1. Send us your ticket. Fill out the form, or text a photo of your citation to 915-200-1133.
  2. We confirm the flat fee. A staff member calls or texts you, confirms the court and citation details and quotes the flat fee — no surprises, no hourly billing.
  3. We fight it. We file our appearance so you don't go to court and we pursue a dismissal, deferred disposition or the best available result — and keep you updated until the case is closed.

Why not just pay it?

Paying a Texas traffic ticket is a plea of guilty or no contest. It becomes a conviction on your driving record that insurers see for years. In most cases our flat fee is less than the fine plus the insurance increase. Read more: Should I Hire a Traffic Ticket Lawyer?

Have a warrant?

Hiring us lets us post an appearance bond to recall the warrant and fight the underlying ticket. See the warrant lawyer page, or let us check for warrants first — it's free.
